This is a translation of the German privacy policy (Datenschutzerklärung).

What the app does

SKS, shown in the app as “SKS Lernapp”, is an offline learning and exam-preparation app. Its learning content is built into the app.

Data stored on your device

When you rate your answer to a question as “Falsch” (wrong), “Teilweise richtig” (partly correct) or “Perfekt” (perfect), the app saves the question ID, your rating, and the date and time of the rating. This learning progress is stored locally on your device so that the app can remember it.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not intentionally collect personal data, and it does not send your learning progress or personal data to us or to anyone else. We therefore have no access to your learning progress.

The app has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, cloud synchronization, server-side user profiles, in-app purchases or payments.

Permissions and device access

The app does not intentionally request or use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, health data or similar device data. The current Android version requests no Android permissions.

Deletion and backups

You can delete all of your learning progress at any time in the app under “Übersicht” → “Alle Fortschrittsdaten löschen”. The built-in learning content remains available. Otherwise, your progress stays on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Depending on your device settings, backups made by your device’s operating system may include the app’s locally stored data. Deleting progress in the app does not necessarily remove it from backups that already exist.

Apple, Google and your operating system

Apple, Google and your device’s operating system may process information independently of the app, for example when you download the app or back up your device, according to their own privacy policies. Depending on your device settings, Apple or Google may also provide us with crash reports and statistics about app use. We use these only to find and fix problems in the app.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking services. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit the website, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver it and, according to GitHub, stores it for security purposes (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This may involve a transfer of data to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). How long GitHub keeps this data is set out in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

The app works the same way for all users, regardless of age.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, where the legal requirements are met. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).
